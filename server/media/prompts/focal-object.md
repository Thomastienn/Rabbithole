Focal object: while the narration explains something, one focal object holds the eye; bring it forward through size, position, highlight or motion, and keep the rest quiet. An overview moment may show the whole scene, unlabelled, so the learner sees how the parts fit. Labels are attention cues, not captions: show a short label only to draw the eye to the focal object, let it echo a key term the narration just said, and fade it once its point has landed, so old labels never crowd the next focal object. The learner is already reading the subtitles, so never put whole narration sentences on screen.
