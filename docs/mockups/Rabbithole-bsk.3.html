Spotlight: what the tutor talks about stays bright and the rest of the page dims

Rabbithole-bsk.3. While the tutor speaks, the board shows the learner where to look: the items its latest sentence drew or named stay lit and every other item on the page dims. Whenever the tutor stops and waits, or the learner pauses or cuts in, the whole page comes back. The spotlight lives only in the board player and the renderer: it changes no saved look, stored board, board pages list, model history, page thumbnail or snapshot, and there is no setting for it. Spec: docs/board-sketches-spec.md (Spotlight). The spec wins where they differ.

  • Dimmed items draw at 30% opacity. They fade in 300 ms and fade back in 300 ms; with reduced motion on, both happen at once.
  • Lit items are the top-level items on the tutor's open page that the latest sentence with ops drew or named. Naming is a highlight, a pointer, a connector's two ends and a child's in or on component. A highlight or pointer names only the item it targets. An erase lights nothing.
  • A child keeps its whole component lit: adding a stage lights the whole cycle, not the new stage alone.
  • A sentence without ops, or with only an erase, keeps the lit set it inherited. The first sentence of a reply that has no ops leaves the page in full until a sentence draws or names something.
  • An item drawn with look=muted keeps its muted colour and is never dimmed further. Learner pen strokes never dim.
  • The whole page shows, with nothing dimmed: at reply end, at a ?> question and a ~> check-in (both end the reply), while paused, from the moment the learner cuts in, on a saved board that is not playing, and on a page the learner opened themselves mid-reply. Page thumbnails and snapshots always show the full page.
  • A highlight wash belongs to its item and dims with it. The pointer is drawn over the board and is never dimmed.
  • Sizes are board units on the fixed 1600×900 board: 6-unit ink marker strokes, 44-unit labels, dashed muted strokes for the orbit. Colours are the theme's: ink text-primary, muted text-secondary, porcelain rabbithole-paper, the learner stroke in the conversation colour.
  • Captions show the sentence being spoken. Frames read left to right, top to bottom.

1. Spotlight on: the tutor speaks about the arrow it just drew

The tutor's fourth sentence drew a2, the arrow from the Moon to the Earth. A connector names its two ends, so the arrow, the Moon and the Earth stay lit. The Sun and the sunlight arrow dim to 30%. The orbit was drawn muted, so it keeps its muted look and is not dimmed further. The learner's pen loop round the Sun, from the question they asked, stays at full strength although the Sun under it is dimmed.

3. Paused: the whole page

The same moment as screen 1, but the learner pressed pause. While the board is paused every item shows in full, so the learner can study the whole drawing. Pressing play brings the spotlight back on the sentence it was paused on.
