Search inside conversations, found from every view

Draft before a ticket; rename to docs/mockups/<issue-id>.html when it is filed. The Ctrl+K window from Rabbithole-nav.2 already searches inside every conversation and branch, but its only button is an unlabelled magnifier beside New chat called "Search conversations", which reads as finding a conversation by its title. This change says what the search covers, and puts a labelled way in on the map, the one view that stays open when the sidebar is a rail or the narrow header is hidden. The window and its results do not change.

  • The map's controls gain a first item: the magnifier, the words "Search tree" and a quiet Ctrl K, then a seam before Zoom out. It opens the same window, where this tree's results already come first.
  • The words stay visible, not only in a tooltip, because the map is where the learner sees many branches and wants to find one.
  • Below 48rem the control drops the shortcut and keeps the icon and "Search", so the controls still fit a 390px screen.
  • The sidebar, rail and narrow header buttons keep their place and icon. Their name and tooltip become "Search inside conversations (Ctrl+K)".
  • The window's field says what it searches, and the empty query adds one line naming every field it reads, above the recent learning trees.
  • The focused chat gets no new button: the sidebar or rail is always beside it on desktop, and Ctrl+K works from the composer.

How do fungi trade?
Mycorrhizal fungi trade phosphorus for sugar. The fungus threads its hyphae between root cells and extends the reach of the plant.
1.1Why trade at all?
Why trade at all?
Neither partner can make what the other offers.
1.2What are hyphae?
What are hyphae?
Hyphae are the thread-like cells a fungus is built from. Each one grows only at its tip.
1.2.1How thin is thin?
How thin is thin?
Two to five micrometres, a fifth of a root hair.
1.2.2What is an arbuscule?
What is an arbuscule?
A finely branched fold of hypha inside a root cell.
