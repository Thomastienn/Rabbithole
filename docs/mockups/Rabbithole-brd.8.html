Board: speed and pause

Rabbithole-brd.8. The board's tool group gains a speed control and a pause button. Speed applies at once to both speech and drawing, without restarting the sentence. Pause stops both, and resume carries on from the same sentence and item. Spec: docs/board-spec.md. Decision: ADR 0028. The spec wins where they differ.

  • The tool group sits at the left of the composer's row, in this order: mic, speed, pause. The pen joins it after the mic in a later ticket. The mic is drawn disabled, as in the Rabbithole-brd.1 frame, because voice arrives in Rabbithole-brd.11.
  • Speed is the composer's 30px pill, at least 56px wide, showing the current speed in tabular figures: "1.5×". It is 1.5× until the learner picks another, and the pick is remembered in this browser, so it is still set after a reload.
  • The speed menu opens upward from the pill, left-aligned, 180px wide, in the shared menu frame. Under the "Speed" label, it lists 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75× and 2×. The current speed has a check, and 1.5× is marked "Default". Picking one closes the menu.
  • Pause is a 36px icon button. While playing, it shows the pause icon and is named "Pause". While paused, it shows the play icon, is named "Resume" and stays pressed in the hover tone. With nothing to play, it is disabled at 45% opacity.
  • While paused, the state label reads "Paused" with a muted dot. The caption holds the paused sentence in ink, and the item being drawn stays exactly where it stopped. Nothing else on the board changes.
  • A reply that is still arriving keeps buffering while paused, with no change on screen. After resume, it plays in full.
  • Speed and pause work the same with the keyboard: Tab reaches them in order, Enter or Space presses them, and the arrow keys move through the open speed menu.

4. Paused

The learner pressed Pause in the middle of sentence three. Speech stops at once and the focus ring stays half drawn, exactly where it stopped. The caption holds the sentence. Pause becomes Resume, pressed. Speed can still be changed, and the reply keeps arriving in the background.

1Why does the Moon have phases?
Paused

5. Resumed

Resume picks up the same sentence and the same item: speech goes on from where it stopped and the ring finishes drawing. Sentences that arrived while paused play next, in full, in order.
