Tutor-guided boards

Rabbithole-brd.13. Learner-led or tutor-guided is an app-wide setting on the Board page of Settings, learner-led by default. After a ?> question the board waits about 4 s. Silence brings one hint, and about 4 s more brings the explanation carrying on. Answers are never graded. Spec: docs/board-spec.md. Decision: ADR 0028. The spec wins where they differ.

  • The only new control is the "How the board runs" row on the Board page of Settings: a two-part segmented control, Learner-led then Tutor-guided, with one hint line under it that describes the chosen option. Learner-led is chosen by default.
  • The row joins the Board page that Rabbithole-brd.12 adds, after the Voice group, which keeps Voice language and Push-to-talk together. Its heading is the 12px caps label every settings group uses.
  • On the board, a question looks like any other tutor sentence: the same caption, the same board items. The wait shows no timer, countdown or prompt, so silence never reads as a test.
  • During the wait the question stays in the caption line in ink, and the state label is empty (idle). It changes only when the hint, the carry-on or the learner's answer replaces it.
  • The hint and the carry-on are ordinary tutor sentences with ordinary board items. Neither mentions the silence.
  • Answers are never graded: no tick, cross, score or wrong look. A reply to an answer that missed simply explains.
  • No-answer turns never show as learner messages, on the board or in the conversation.

1. Settings: the Board page, learner-led by default

The Board page from Rabbithole-brd.12 gains a row after Voice. Learner-led is chosen until the learner picks otherwise. The hint under the control describes the chosen option. The second hint names the spoken or typed switch for one board.

2. Settings: tutor-guided chosen

The choice applies to every board from the next board turn, and the board prompt tells the tutor which one applies. The hint line swaps to describe tutor-guided.

How the board runs

Learner-ledTutor-guided

3. The tutor asks: 0 s

The tutor has drawn a hill and a tangent on its rising side, then speaks a ?> line. The question stays in the caption in ink. The state is idle, and the board waits about 4 s. The mic is off here; with it on, the learner can just answer aloud.

4. Silence: one hint, about 4 s later

The session sends a no-answer turn, and the tutor gives one hint. Here it marks the hilltop with a dot in the focus look. The hint is a short sentence that never mentions the silence. The board waits about 4 s again after it.

about 4 s · hint

Speaking

Look at the very top. Which way would a line touching it tilt?

5. Still silent: the explanation carries on, about 4 s after the hint

A second no-answer turn has the tutor give the answer and carry on teaching, with no comment on the silence. It draws the flat tangent and its label in the focus look, then moves on.

6. An answer within the wait cancels the hint

The learner starts typing during the wait (or speaks, with the mic on). Typing holds the wait open until they send. Their answer is an ordinary learner message and counts as familiarity evidence. No hint comes: the tutor replies to the answer and goes on.

7. An answer that misses is never graded

The learner answered "still positive?". The tutor explains from where they are, with no wrong look, tick or cross, and the board gets the same items as when the answer landed. Shown as the caption line only; the board is as in 6.

Speaking

Just before the top it still climbs. Right at the top it lies flat, so the slope is zero.

8. Switching one board by asking

The learner types or says it on a learner-led board, and the tutor starts checking in from its next part. "Stop asking" on a tutor-guided board does the reverse. Only this board changes: the setting stays as it is, and the model handles the request itself. Shown as caption line and composer only.

9. In the conversation: no-answer turns leave no learner message

Each no-answer turn is a system turn. The question, the hint and the carry-on are saved as tutor replies one after another, with no learner message between them, and the silence counts for nothing in familiarity. The normal chat view shows them as board replies, each with its snapshot.
