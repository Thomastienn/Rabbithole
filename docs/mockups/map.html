1How do fungi trade?
How do fungi trade with plants?Trading partners. Mycorrhizal fungi trade phosphorus for sugar. The fungus threads its hyphae between root cells and extends the plant's reach. Diagram: Trade loop
1.1Why trade at all?
Why trade at all?Neither partner can make what the other offers. A plant fixes carbon from air and light, but its roots touch only a thin sleeve of soil.
1.2What are hyphae?
What are hyphae?Thread-like cells, a tenth the width of a root hair, that run for centimetres through the soil.
