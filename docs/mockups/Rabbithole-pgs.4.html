Board: the page strip and the back chip

Rabbithole-pgs.4. A board holds titled pages and shows one at a time. A thin strip above the board lists every page's title; clicking one opens that page. A page made from another shows a back chip naming that page. The board frame (toggle, board, caption line, composer, mic, speed and pause) is Rabbithole-brd.1's and brd.8's, the transcript rail is brd.15's and the reply stepper is brd.4's. Spec: docs/board-pages-and-check-ins-spec.md. Decision: ADR 0029. The spec wins where they differ.

  • The strip is its own 36px row above the board, 8px from it, left-aligned with the board and as wide as it. The board stays 16:9 in what is left of the field. The row is kept on an empty board, so nothing moves when the first page opens.
  • Each title is a 30px button: Barlow 13px/500 in muted ink, 10px side padding, 6px corners, 2px apart, at most 200px wide with an ellipsis. Hover: the raised fill (hover token) and ink.
  • The page on screen is marked: ink, weight 600 and a 2px porcelain bar at the button's bottom edge, 10px in from each side. It carries aria-current="page". The strip is a nav labelled "Board pages".
  • Order is creation order with each page right after its parent, depth first. Every page but the first came from another page and starts with the from mark: arrow-turn-down-right at 14px, 4px before the title. The mark does not show depth.
  • A page with no title shows as "Page N", N being its place in creation order.
  • When titles overflow, the strip scrolls sideways with no scrollbar and centres the marked title where it can, so it is always in view. Each edge with more titles beyond it fades out over 48px.
  • The back chip sits over the board at the top left, 12px from the top and 16px from the left, opposite the reply stepper: 36px tall, popover fill, 1px border, 8px corners, the arrow-uturn-left icon at 16px in muted ink, then "Back to" and the parent's title in Barlow 13px/500 ink. At most 45% of the board wide, with an ellipsis. Hover: hover fill. The first page has none.
  • Clicking a title or the back chip opens that page with the same slide as a tutor's open (a cross-fade with reduced motion). While the tutor draws, its next item brings the view back to its page; while it is idle, the learner's page stays.
  • Default chosen in this mockup, open to the owner: stepped back to an earlier reply, the strip lists only the pages that existed after that reply.

The board is on, but nothing has been asked on it yet. The strip row is there and holds no titles, so the board does not move or shrink when the first page opens. The rail shows the chat turns from before the board.

What are hyphae?

Hyphae are the thread-like cells a fungus is built from. Each one is a tube a few micrometres wide that grows only at its tip and branches as it goes.

Together the threads form the mycelium, a network that can run for metres through soil. Because a thread is so thin, it reaches pores no root can enter.

What are hyphae?

Hyphae are the thread-like cells a fungus is built from. Each one is a tube a few micrometres wide that grows only at its tip and branches as it goes.

Together the threads form the mycelium, a network that can run for metres through soil. Because a thread is so thin, it reaches pores no root can enter.

How does a hypha grow at its tip?

A hypha grows only at its very tip. Tiny sacs called vesicles carry new wall material forward. At the tip, enzymes soften the wall. Pressure inside pushes the soft tip outward. Then the new wall hardens behind it, and the tip moves on.
