Update banner in the desktop app

Rabbithole-upd.2. On launch the desktop app checks for a newer published release once. When there is one, a small card in the sidebar offers it: one click to download, one click to restart into it. Only the desktop app shows it, never npm start. Spec: docs/add-ons-updates-and-video-mode-spec.md. Where this mockup and the spec differ, follow the spec.

  • The card sits in the sidebar above Settings, where the save alert already lives. It never covers the reading column and never opens a dialog.
  • One action at a time: Install, then Restart to update, or Retry after a failed download.
  • If the app is offline or the check fails, nothing is shown. A failed download shows a short reason and Retry.
  • The × hides the card until the next launch. Downloading continues if it has started. (Mockup addition, not in the spec.)

1.2What are hyphae?

Tutor · Claude · Opus 4

A hypha is a single thread of a fungus, a tube one cell wide. Thousands of them together make the mycelium, the web you see as white fuzz under a log.

Because a thread is so thin, it reaches pores no root can enter, and that is where much of the phosphorus sits.
