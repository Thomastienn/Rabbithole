Board: the transcript rail, and branching from it

Rabbithole-brd.15. A narrow rail beside the board shows what was said, captioning the current sentence as it plays. The learner can collapse it, and the one-line caption under the board shows instead. Selecting a passage in the rail branches as in chat: the board pauses and the child opens in normal chat without a board. The board frame (toggle, board, caption line, composer and mic) is Rabbithole-brd.1's. Spec: docs/board-spec.md. Decision: ADR 0028. The spec wins where they differ.

  • The rail is 300px wide, right of the board, below the 52px header. It runs the full height of the board field, the caption line and the composer, with a 1px seam on its left. The board stays 16:9 and as large as the narrower field allows.
  • The rail head is 44px tall with a 1px seam below: the "Transcript" caps label, 16px from the left, then the collapse button (chevron right) at the right. While the board is paused, "Paused" shows before the button in muted Barlow 12px.
  • The rail reads top to bottom like the conversation, and keeps the newest line in view. A learner question is a raised bubble at the right, at most 236px wide, in Barlow 14px/1.4 with 8px by 12px padding and 8px corners. A reply is the reading face at 15px/1.55, 12px between items, 16px padding.
  • Reply text in the rail is muted. Only the sentence being spoken is porcelain. A sentence appears when it starts playing; sentences not yet spoken never show.
  • Collapsed, the rail is a 44px strip with only the Show transcript button (chevron left) at the top. The current sentence then shows as the one-line caption under the board. Open, the caption cell is empty and the caption line keeps only the session state, so the board does not move.
  • Branching uses the chat's own pieces: the selection colour, Ask about selection (small primary button), and the passage box with Send, Explain briefly and Ask here. Keyboard: hold Shift and use the arrow keys in the rail, then Tab to the button.
  • Send pauses the board and opens the child in normal chat without a board. A branched passage keeps the chat's mark in the rail: the underline in the child's line colour, its ring and its chip.
  • This mockup opens the rail by default. The spec does not say which way a board opens, or whether the choice is remembered.

1. Rail open: the current sentence plays in the rail

The rail lists the conversation's questions and replies, the chat turns before the board included. The sentence being spoken appears at the end in porcelain as it starts, and the board draws its items. Earlier sentences turn muted. The caption line keeps only the session state, so the board does not change size when the rail opens or closes.

What are hyphae?

Hyphae are the thread-like cells a fungus is built from. Each one is a tube a few micrometres wide that grows only at its tip and branches as it goes.

Together the threads form the mycelium, a network that can run for metres through soil. Because a thread is so thin, it reaches pores no root can enter.

How does a hypha grow at its tip?

A hypha grows only at its very tip. Tiny sacs called vesicles carry new wall material forward. At the tip, enzymes soften the wall.

2. Rail collapsed: the one-line caption under the board

The collapse button shrinks the rail to a 44px strip holding only Show transcript. The board grows into the space, and the current sentence shows as the one-line caption, as in Rabbithole-brd.1. Opening the rail again moves the caption back into it.

3. Selecting a passage in the rail

Any text in the rail, a question or a reply, can be selected as in chat. Selecting it offers Ask about selection under the paragraph. The tutor keeps speaking and the rail keeps adding sentences below; the rail does not scroll away while a selection is held.

4. The passage box, as in chat

Ask about selection opens the chat's passage box at the rail's width: the question field with Send, then Explain briefly and Ask here. The board keeps playing while the learner types. Send creates the focused chat; Escape or a click outside closes the box.

Send branches as in chat: the child conversation 1.2.1 opens in normal chat with its Board toggle off, and the map comes back with the new branch. The parent's board pauses at the sentence it had reached; its speech and drawing stop at once and nothing more is spoken there. The learner can turn the child's board on from its own toggle.
