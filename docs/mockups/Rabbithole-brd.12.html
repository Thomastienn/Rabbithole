Board settings: Voice language and push-to-talk

Rabbithole-brd.12. Settings gains a Board page. Voice language sets the language the learner speaks and the tutor speaks back, English by default. Push-to-talk replaces hands-free listening with holding Space or the mic button, off by default. Spec: docs/board-spec.md. Decision: ADR 0028. The spec wins where they differ.

  • Board is a new page in the Settings nav, after Models, with the speaker icon. Its heading is "Board", described as "How the tutor talks and listens on a board." It is laid out like every other Settings page.
  • The page has one section, Voice, with two controls. Voice language is a dropdown, at most 300px wide, because the voice table has more than five languages. Push-to-talk is a switch with its description. Learner-led or tutor-guided joins the page in Rabbithole-brd.13.
  • Voice language lists English first, then the other languages of the voice table by name. Under it, a hint says what the setting does. Picking a language other than English adds one line saying how large its speech model is and that it downloads on the next mic press. Once that model is on this computer, the line goes away.
  • There is no voice picker. The tutor speaks the voice language in that language's one voice from the voice table.
  • With push-to-talk on, the mic on the board is a hold button, never a toggle, and nothing listens until it is held. It reads "Hold to talk" with the Space key beside it. While Space or the button is held, the tutor stops, the button fills porcelain, and it reads "Release to send" with the level bars. Letting go sends what was said. Voice detection never cuts in.
  • Space holds to talk whenever focus is not in a text field. In the composer, Space types a space, and the learner holds the mic button instead.
  • The first hold downloads the speech models, with the same progress on the button as Rabbithole-brd.11, and records nothing until they are ready.

7. Held

The learner holds Space. The tutor stops at once and the focus ring snaps to finished. The button fills porcelain, shows the learner's level and reads "Release to send". The state reads Listening.

8. Released: question sent

Letting go sends what was said while held, with no wait for silence. The button goes back to "Hold to talk", and the question shows muted while the tutor thinks.
