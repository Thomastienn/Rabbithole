SketchesReadskt.1 and skt.7 Sketches

Sketches

Rabbithole-skt.1 (flow and bars in replies) and Rabbithole-skt.7 (Expand into visual). A ```sketch <kind> block draws as a small diagram on the reply's own ground, the moment its fence closes. Spec: docs/sketches-spec.md. Decision: ADR 0027. The spec wins where they differ.

  • A sketch has no frame, fill or head band. It sits in the reading column, at most 640px wide, 8px below the paragraph before it and 20px above the next.
  • No line colours. The four lines belong to branches, so a sketch uses only ink, muted ink, the strong seam and porcelain.
  • Flow is a small line diagram. It has 3px strong-seam lines with round caps that turn only at 45 or 90 degrees, 12px muted station rings, and a muted chevron on each step. A fork drops to a row 84px lower and rejoins with a 45 degree turn.
  • Bars are 8px round-capped strong-seam lines in rows 26px apart. The longest bar spans the track (360px at most) minus 72px left for its value. A zero value is a dot.
  • The focus words, wrapped in *stars*, are porcelain: a filled station or bar, and a 600-weight label. Everything else is ink.
  • The tutor wrote every word, so all text is in the reading face. The optional title is 15px/600. Station and bar labels are 14px. Arrow labels are 13px muted.
  • A sketch is not a figure. It has no "Fig N", it is not in the Figures list and it has no copy button. Its source never shows.
  • Its controls show only on hover or keyboard focus inside the sketch (always where the device cannot hover). They sit in a raised box at the top right, like a visual's.

In a reply

One sketch per section that has a shape, right after the text it shows. Both are at rest: no controls until pointed at.

Counting arrangements

Flip four coins and write down the order: there are 16 possible sequences. Most of them land in the middle. There is only one way to get no heads, but six ways to get exactly two.

Entropy counts those ways. A state that can happen in more ways is more likely, which is why mixed results win.

Why freezing water does not break the rule

Ice is more ordered than water, so the water's own entropy falls as it freezes. But freezing releases heat, and that heat spreads into colder surroundings, which gain more entropy than the water lost.

So the total still rises. The second law is about the whole system, never one part of it.

Flow: steps, with arrow labels and no title

Each rank is a column: 640px divided by the number of ranks. Stations are centred in their column. A label wraps at the column width minus 24px, and an arrow label sits above the middle of its step. With no title, the drawing starts at the top, and on hover the controls box sits over its top-right corner, as it does over a visual today.

```sketch flow
Rock -> Loose sand: breaks and moves
Loose sand -> Buried layers: settles
Buried layers -> *Sandstone*: squeezed, glued
```

Flow: branch and rejoin

A step's rank is its longest path from a start. A second child forks onto the next row down. A step that skips ranks runs straight along its row, and a rejoin merges into that run before the target, so both share one chevron.

Shown hovered: the controls box appears at the top right and covers nothing the title row needs.

```sketch flow
title: Freezing below 0 °C
Water freezes -> Water's entropy falls
Water freezes -> Heat flows out
Heat flows out -> Surroundings' entropy rises more
Water's entropy falls -> *Total rises*
Surroundings' entropy rises more -> *Total rises*
```

Bars: with a unit, and a zero

The labels take the width they need, up to 45% of the column. The unit follows the number. A zero value is an 8px dot, so the row still reads as a bar.

```sketch bars
title: Approximate eastward speed of the ground
Equator: 465 m/s
30° latitude: 403 m/s
*60° latitude*: 233 m/s
North Pole: 0 m/s
```

Expand into visual (Rabbithole-skt.7)

Expand asks for a normal visual, built from the sketch's title, its items and the text around it. The visual takes the sketch's place in the reply. From then on it is an ordinary visual: it counts in the Figures list and has visual parts, new versions and Enlarge. The sketch stays one menu item away.

1. Point at the sketch

The box shows one control, "Expand into visual". Tab into the sketch to reach it, and press Enter to expand.

2. Drawing, in its place

The sketch gives way at once to today's drawing state for a visual: the timer badge over a 180px shimmer. The paragraphs around it do not move.

Entropy counts those ways.

3. The visual, with the sketch one menu item away

The visual is drawn as any visual is today, with Ask and the actions menu. The menu gains "Show sketch" after the version items, only on a visual expanded from a sketch. This is the same way "Previous version" goes back to a replaced version.

Entropy counts those ways.

4. Back on the sketch

The sketch draws again in the same place. Its control now reads "Show visual" and brings the same visual back, with no new request. When the conversation reopens, the reply shows whichever of the two was showing last.
