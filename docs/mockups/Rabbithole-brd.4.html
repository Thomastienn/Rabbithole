Math, highlight, pointer, erase and fresh space

Rabbithole-brd.4. The second reply on the torch board points, highlights, labels with math, erases what it is done with, then moves to fresh space and writes a formula term by term. The learner can scroll back to the earlier area and step back to the board as it stood after the first reply. Spec: docs/board-spec.md (Op language, Board player, Saving). Decision: ADR 0028. The spec wins where they differ. Frame and shapes are Rabbithole-brd.1's and brd.2's.

  • Math is KaTeX in its own faces, centred on its point, in the look's colour: size=s 32px, m 44px (the default), l 64px. A term is one top-level group: a symbol, an operator, a fraction, a root. Terms write in left to right, one after another, sharing the op's time equally.
  • A highlight is a porcelain wash at 22% under its item, 20px past the item's box on every side, with 28px corners. It sweeps in left to right over its share and stays until erased.
  • The pointer is a filled porcelain cursor, 62×88 board pixels, with a 3px edge in the board's ground. Its tip stops 12px outside its item's edge, at the item's middle. It glides there in the first half of its share, from where it last stopped (the first time, the board's centre), rests, then fades over the share's last 0.5 s.
  • Erase fades every named item out together over its share. The space they leave is free again.
  • Clear pans the camera down one whole area (900 board pixels) over its share, easing in and out. Coordinates in the new area start at 0,0 again. A 2px seam line marks where an area ends; earlier areas stay on the board.
  • The board scrolls vertically through every area, with a thin thumb at the right edge. Once the board has two or more replies, a stepper at its top right steps through the board as it stood after each reply.
