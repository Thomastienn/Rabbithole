Map inks: fork inks and depth stops

Throwaway mockup. The same learning tree drawn under each rule: a trunk with five children, a fork of three under 1.2, a fork of four under 1.2.2, and one chain down to depth 9.

  • A. Today. Each first-level family takes line 1 to 4 in order (1.5 repeats line 1); every descendant keeps its family line.
  • B. Fork inks. Children of the trunk take 1, 2, 3, 4, 1 in order. Under a parent on line p, children take the three other lines in ascending order, then p, then repeat. An only child keeps its parent’s line.
  • C. Fork inks and depth stops. B, plus a stop row under the address chip: one stop per level, no cap, the last filled. This is the chosen design.
  • D. Cap considered and rejected. The depth-9 card with nine stops (chosen) against the capped row (rejected).
