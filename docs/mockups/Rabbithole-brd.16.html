Board replies fit the rest of the app

Rabbithole-brd.16. Everything said on a board is an ordinary reply in the conversation. In the normal chat view a board reply shows its spoken text and a small snapshot of the board at that reply, rendered from its ops. Familiarity, concepts and usage treat board turns like chat turns, with no new look. The board frame (toggle, board, caption line, composer and mic) is Rabbithole-brd.1's. Spec: docs/board-spec.md. Decision: ADR 0028. The spec wins where they differ.

  • A board reply in normal chat is drawn as any reply: the learner's question bubble, the "Tutor · model" line, the text, the reply footer. Its text is the spoken sentences, joined with spaces into one paragraph in the reading face at 17px/1.68.
  • The snapshot sits right after the text, 4px below it and 18px above the footer. It is 400px wide at 16:9 (225px tall), never wider than the column, on the deep board surface with 8px corners and no border, frame or header.
  • It is the board at the end of that reply: the replay of every op up to it, earlier replies' items included, in the camera area showing when the reply ended. It is drawn still in the board's own looks, scaled from 1600×900, with no animation and no controls.
  • A reply left halfway shows only the sentences that started playing, and its snapshot only their ops.
  • Board replies get no suggestion marks and no Next questions. The learner's questions on a board, spoken or typed, show as ordinary question bubbles.
  • A screen reader hears the snapshot as one image, "Board at this reply:" followed by its text labels in order.
  • Familiarity, concepts and usage look as they do today and are not drawn here. The quick thinking model judges familiarity and records concepts after each board reply, so concepts reach the Knowledge view as they do from chat. The learner's board questions count as familiarity evidence. Board turns count as Chat replies in Usage, under the conversation's chat model and its tree.

1. Board replies in the normal chat view

With the Board toggle off, 1.2 reads as an ordinary conversation: an earlier chat turn, then two board turns. Each board reply is its spoken sentences as one paragraph, then a 400px snapshot of the board as it stood at the end of that reply, then the usual reply footer. Board replies carry no suggestion marks and no next questions. The map node lists the board turns like any other.

What are hyphae?
Tutor · Claude · Sonnet 4.5

Hyphae are the thread-like cells a fungus is built from. Each one is a tube a few micrometres wide that grows only at its tip and branches as it goes.

Together the threads form the mycelium, a network that can run for metres through soil. Because a thread is so thin, it reaches pores no root can enter.

How does a hypha grow at its tip?
Tutor · Claude · Sonnet 4.5

A hypha grows only at its very tip. Tiny sacs called vesicles carry new wall material forward. At the tip, enzymes soften the wall. Pressure inside pushes the soft tip outward. Then the new wall hardens behind it, and the tip moves on.

Why can’t the older wall stretch too?
Tutor · Claude · Sonnet 4.5

Because it has already hardened. Only the soft tip can give way. So all the growth happens at the front.
