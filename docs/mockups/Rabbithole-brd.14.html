The learner draws on the board with a pen

Rabbithole-brd.14. A pen in the board's tool group lets the learner draw, circle and point. A finished stroke is hit-tested against the board items, and the next question carries references such as "circled t2". A stroke over no item goes as a cropped image, only when the chat model reads images. Drawing alone sends nothing. Spec: docs/board-spec.md. Decision: ADR 0028. The spec wins where they differ.

  • The pen is a 36px icon button in the composer's tool group, right after the mic. It is a toggle: pressed, it fills porcelain with the icon in the porcelain text colour, as the mic does when on. Its label reads "Pen" in both states, with aria-pressed.
  • With the pen on, the pointer over the board is a crosshair and a drag draws a stroke. Off, the board ignores the pointer. The pen stays as the learner left it after a question is sent, and it is off when the board opens.
  • Learner strokes are freehand, 6 board px wide with round caps and joins, in the conversation's family ink (the header chip's colour). The tutor's items never use that ink, so the learner's marks are always told apart.
  • Strokes wait on the board. A pill above the composer text says how many marks go with the next question, with a short line in the learner's ink. A stroke over no item says it goes as an image, or, when the chat model does not read images, that it stays on the board only. The pill is muted then, with an information icon.
  • Drawing never cuts the tutor off: it keeps speaking until the learner sends a spoken or typed question. Sending clears the pill. The strokes stay on the board until the tutor erases them, which they do with a fade like any erased item.
  • A learner message saves its strokes and references, so the board reopens with every stroke the tutor did not erase.

1. Pen off, the tutor speaking

The tool group holds the mic and the pen. Nothing on the board responds to the pointer.

8. Reopened: the learner's strokes come back

The board reopens as it was at the end of the latest reply. The sketch was saved with the learner's message and the tutor kept it, so it shows again; the erased marks do not. The pen is off, as on every opening.
