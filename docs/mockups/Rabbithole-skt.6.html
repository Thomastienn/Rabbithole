Ask about a sketch item

Rabbithole-skt.6. Every drawn item of a sketch can be pointed at and asked about, the way a visual part can. Asking opens a branch from that item, carrying the sketch's title and the item's label. It reuses the passage box, the crumbs of a picked visual part, the branched part's mark and the visual's chip row. Spec: docs/sketches-spec.md. Decision: ADR 0027. The spec wins where they differ.

  • An item is one drawn element with a label: a flow station or a bars row. Arrows and arrow labels belong to their step and are not items of their own.
  • Flow hit area: the station's column width minus 8px (152px when there are 4 ranks), centred on the station. It runs from 12px above the station's centre to 8px below the last baseline of the tallest label in its row. With rows 84px apart, row hit areas keep at least 18px between them.
  • Bars hit area: the whole row, from the label to the end of the bar track, which holds the value. It is 26px tall (3px above and below the 20px line) and reaches 6px past each side. Rows abut, so a pointer anywhere on the sketch's rows picks exactly one row.
  • Pointing: the cursor becomes a pointer and a 2px dotted muted-ink outline with 4px corners appears inside the hit area. It fades in over 120ms. This is the outline a visual part shows, without its name tag, because the label is already drawn. The sketch's controls box appears as in skt.1.
  • Keyboard: a sketch's items are one tab stop, before its controls box. Tab lands on the item focused last, else the first. The arrow keys move through the items in the order the screen reader reads them: Right and Down go to the next item, Left and Up to the previous one. Home and End go to the first and last item. These are the keys that pick a visual part. The focused item wears the one focus ring: 2px porcelain, 2px outside the hit area, 6px corners. Enter or Space opens the ask box.
  • Ask box: the passage box, opened as a picked visual part opens it. It has raised enamel, a 1.5px strong seam, 8px corners, 8px padding and 6px gaps, and is at most 400px wide. It opens 4px below the hit area. Its left edge meets the hit area's left edge, except for an item that starts in the right half of the column: then its right edge meets the hit area's right edge. With no room above the composer, it sits 4px above the hit area. The field takes focus.
  • In the box, the crumbs come first: the sketch's title in muted 13px, a 16px chevron, then the item's label in porcelain 14px/600. An untitled sketch reads "Sketch". Next comes the 36px field "Ask about label" with the 36px porcelain Send. Last come Explain briefly and Ask here, both 30px. While the box is open, the item keeps its dotted outline. Escape or a click outside closes the box. Escape also returns focus to the item.
  • Send opens the branch. It carries the sketch's title and the item's label. The box first shows Preparing context…, then the new branch opens at once and its reply streams. Its origin line reads From parent at “title › label”.
  • Back in the parent, a branched item wears a 2px solid outline in its branch's line colour inside the hit area, the way a branched visual part does. This is the only line colour on a sketch. Pointing at the item still opens a new ask box. The item's address chip sits in a row 6px under the sketch, with 4px between chips, and opens the branch.
  • Touch, where the device cannot hover: nothing shows at rest, and the controls box always shows (skt.1). A tap on an item opens the box at once, with the dotted outline on that item. The hit areas stay the same size: a bars row is 26px tall, above the 24px minimum target, and a flow item is at least 48px tall.

1. At rest

Nothing marks an item as askable until it is pointed at or focused. The sketches look exactly as in skt.1.

2. Pointing at a flow station

The pointer is on "Heat flows out". Its hit area shows the dotted outline, and the controls box appears at the top right. A click opens the ask box (state 5).

3. Pointing at a bars row

4. Keyboard focus

Tab into the sketch, and its first item takes the focus ring. The arrow keys then move the ring item by item. Focus inside the sketch also shows its controls box, which is the next Tab stop. Here the ring has moved to "Water's entropy falls" and to "4 heads".

5. The ask box on a flow station

A click on "Heat flows out", or Enter on it, opens the box 4px under its hit area, hanging from the hit area's left edge. The crumbs name the sketch and the item, as a picked visual part's trail does. The field has focus, and a question is typed. Like the passage box, it floats over the text below and moves nothing.

9. The new branch opens

The branch opens at once, the way any new branch does, and its reply streams in. Its origin line quotes the sketch's title and the item's label, joined as the crumbs were. The context the tutor received holds both.

What a screen reader hears

Each item reads as in skt.1, now as a button in the sketch's list. The ask box reads as a visual part's does, with "sketch item" in place of "visual part". A branched item adds its address, and the chip row is named the way a visual's is.
