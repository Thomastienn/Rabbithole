TreeTutor redesign mockup

Paperback Series, Seasonal Inks. A static, non-functional picture of every screen and component. Links and controls do nothing except move between screens. Tick Component labels in the black bar to see each part's name, and Light for the light appearance.

The idea in four rules

  • A learning tree is a volume. Its root conversation, the trunk, wears paper.
  • Each first-level branch family takes one series ink, in order: amber, leaf, rust, lichen, then repeats. The passage underline, its address chip, the branch line and the conversation node band all share that ink.
  • Conversations carry addresses (1, 1.1, 1.1.2). The contents outline, the passage chips and the map all use them.
  • The reply owns the centre. Leaf green is the only action colour, paper is focus, rose red is failure only.

Shared foundation, do first

TokensThe :root block in mock.css holds every colour as light-dark(light, dark), the type faces, radii and the 8px module. It becomes the Astryx theme that replaces src/themes/slate/.
ShellBand (global: tree switcher, view tabs, search, New chat, Settings) over a workspace. It replaces the left rail in src/ChatPage.tsx and src/layout.ts.
FontsArchivo 400 to 700 for interface, Literata 400, 400 italic and 600 for replies. The mockup loads them from Google Fonts; the app must self-host them.
IconsHeroicons 24 outline, already a dependency. icons.css is generated for the mockup only.

What moved, and what did not

  • The saved-tree rail became the Library page and the band's tree switcher. The left column now shows the open tree's contents.
  • The map docks beside the reading column in Read, and takes the whole workspace in Map.
  • Settings is a page, not a dialog. Usage stays a Settings section.
  • Visuals and videos stay borderless and untitled inside replies, as docs/inline-visuals-spec.md requires; their names appear only in the contents under Figures.
  • No behaviour is removed. Every label, action and failure state in the current app has a place in these screens.
