1How do fungi trade?
How do fungi trade?
Mycorrhizal fungi trade phosphorus for sugar. The fungus threads its hyphae between root cells and extends the reach of the plant.
Is the trade fair?
Mostly. Each side can send more to the partners that give more back, which keeps cheating rare.
1.1Why trade at all?
Why trade at all?
Neither partner can make what the other offers. A plant fixes carbon from air and light, but its roots touch only a thin sleeve of soil.
1.2What are hyphae?
What are hyphae?
Hyphae are the thread-like cells a fungus is built from. Each one is a tube a few micrometres wide that grows only at its tip.
Show me how a hypha grows
A hypha lengthens only at its last few micrometres. Vesicles carry wall material forward and the wall softens.

1.2.1How thin is thin?
How thin is thin?
Two to five micrometres, a fifth of a root hair. That is thin enough to enter pores that hold water against gravity.
1.2.2What is an arbuscule?
What is an arbuscule?
An arbuscule is a finely branched fold of hypha inside a root cell
