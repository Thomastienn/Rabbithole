What are hyphae?
Tutor · Claude · Opus 4

Hyphae are the thread-like cells a fungus is built from. Each one is a tube a few micrometres wide1.2.1 that grows only at its tip.

Together the threads form the mycelium, a network that can run for metres through soil.

1How do fungi trade?
How do fungi trade?
Mycorrhizal fungi trade phosphorus for sugar. The fungus threads its hyphae between root cells and extends the reach of the plant.
1.1Why trade at all?
Why trade at all?
Neither partner can make what the other offers. A plant fixes carbon from air and light, but its roots touch only a thin sleeve of soil.
