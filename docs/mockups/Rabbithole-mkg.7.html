Stats panel under the knowledge graph

Rabbithole-mkg.7. Nothing on the Knowledge page shows how knowledge grows. A panel under the graph has five sections: concepts met and known over time, topics by familiarity, fading concepts, misconceptions, and daily activity. Spec: docs/memory-recall-and-knowledge-graph-spec.md, "Stats panel". Where this mockup and the spec differ, follow the spec.

  • The panel reads like the rest of the page: plain sections on the ground, split by seams. It has no cards, big numbers or scores.
  • Familiarity is said in the graph's own words (New to you, Getting there, You know this), never as a percentage or a grade.
  • Every concept name in the panel is a button. Choosing one scrolls back to the graph, selects the concept there and opens its detail.
  • The activity heatmap shows messages per day and nothing more. It has no streak count, goal or reminder.
  • Topic swatches use the same hues as the graph (Rabbithole-mkg.6). Swatches are short line lengths, as in a key.
  • Each chart has a text alternative: the chart's label sums up the trend, and a visually hidden table holds the figures.
