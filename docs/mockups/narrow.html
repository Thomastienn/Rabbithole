11.1 Why trade at all?

Why trade at all?

Why trade at all?

Neither partner can make what the other offers. A plant fixes carbon from air and light, but its roots touch only a thin sleeve of soil. Could either manage without the other1.1.1? Rarely, and poorly.

The plant pays in sugar, up to a fifth of what it makes.
