The tutor draws shapes and labels as it speaks

Rabbithole-brd.2. Each spoken sentence brings one small change to the board: its op lines draw circles, boxes, dots, lines, arrows and short labels at the coordinates the tutor gave, each item entering over its share of the sentence. Spec: docs/board-spec.md (Op language, Board player, UI). Decision: ADR 0028. The spec wins where they differ. The frame around the board is Rabbithole-brd.1's and does not change here.

  • The board is 1600×900 board pixels, drawn as large as the board field allows, as in Rabbithole-brd.1. Every size below is in board pixels and scales with the board (at 912px wide, 1 board pixel is 0.57px).
  • Shapes are clean marker strokes: 7px, round caps and joins, no fill, no jitter. A box has 12px rounded corners. A dot is a filled circle of radius 14. An arrow's head is two 7px strokes, 24px back along the shaft and 18px either side.
  • Labels are in the reading face, weight 500, centred on their point: size=s 32px, m 44px (the default), l 64px. A label is a few words at most.
  • Colour comes only from looks. No look is ink; focus is porcelain, muted muted ink, good success, wrong error, new info. The model never writes a colour.
  • Nothing pops in. A stroke draws on from its start, a dot grows from its centre, a label writes in left to right. Each item takes an equal share of its sentence, in the order the ops came, and eases out (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)).
  • A sentence lasts its estimated time: 15 characters per second at 1.5×. A 57-character sentence lasts 3.8 s.
  • The learner never sees op text, ids or errors on the board or in the caption.

6. Looks, in dark and light

Five looks plus none, the same in every theme by role: none is ink, focus is porcelain, muted is muted ink, good is success, wrong is error and new is info. Shapes and labels use the look's colour for stroke, fill and text alike.

7. A theme switch redraws the board

Switching theme or appearance redraws every item at once in the new theme's colours, in its finished state. Nothing replays its entry. Shown: the same board in four themes.

8. A broken op is skipped

An op that doesn't parse, or names an id the board doesn't have, is left out and logged. Everything else in the reply draws as usual, in its own sentence's time; the skipped op's share goes to the ops left in its sentence. Here the bulb's label had no Y and an arrow named batt: the board simply has no "bulb" label and no third arrow.

9. Reopened later: the final board at once

The reply stored its op lines. Switching the board off and on, or coming back to the conversation, replays every op up to the latest reply and shows the final board in the first frame: no entries animate. The state is idle, and the reply's last sentence is held in muted ink, as in Rabbithole-brd.1.
