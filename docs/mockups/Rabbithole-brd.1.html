Board: open it, type a question, read the captions

Rabbithole-brd.1, the tracer bullet. A Board toggle in the focused chat header switches the conversation to its board. The learner types a question and reads the tutor's sentences one at a time as a caption under the board. Nothing is drawn or spoken aloud yet. This page also sets the board frame every later board mockup copies. Spec: docs/board-spec.md. Decision: ADR 0028. The spec wins where they differ.

  • The Board toggle is a 36px tall control in the focused chat header, first in the row of controls at the right: the 36×20 switch, 8px gap, then "Board" in Barlow 500 13px. Muted when off, ink when on.
  • While the board is open, the map is hidden and the board column takes all the width right of the sidebar. The column has four fixed rows: the 52px header, the board field, the 52px caption line and the composer. State never moves them.
  • The board is a fixed 1600×900 surface (ADR 0028) drawn at 16:9 on the deep field, with 8px corners and no border. It is as large as the field allows, centred, with 16px above it and 24px at each side.
  • The empty board says only "Ask a question to start." in muted Barlow 15px, centred. It shows only before the conversation's first board question.
  • The caption is one line in the reading face, 17px/1.5, centred under the board. The current tutor sentence is ink. The sent question while thinking, and the last sentence once the reply ends, are muted. A sentence too long for the line ends in an ellipsis.
  • The session state sits at the left of the caption line in the 12px caps label: "Thinking" with a muted dot, "Speaking" with a porcelain dot. Idle shows nothing.
  • The composer is the chat composer, centred, at most 720px wide, with no model, effort or attach controls: a board uses the conversation's chat model at low effort. Its tool group holds the mic, which is off when a board opens; speed, pause and pen join it in later tickets. Here the mic is disabled, because voice arrives in Rabbithole-brd.11.
  • A notice is one line at the top centre of the board, over it, in the shared frame: raised enamel, 8px corners, 1.5px strong seam, Barlow 13px. A failure notice uses the scarlet wash with an icon and words that say what to do.

1. Normal chat, Board off

The focused chat header gains the Board toggle, first among its controls: a switch and the word Board. Everything else in the chat view is as it is today.

What are hyphae?
Tutor · Claude · Sonnet 4.5

Hyphae are the thread-like cells a fungus is built from. Each one is a tube a few micrometres wide that grows only at its tip and branches as it goes.

Together the threads form the mycelium, a network that can run for metres through soil. Because a thread is so thin, it reaches pores no root can enter.

3. Thinking

The learner typed a question and sent it. The composer clears, Send is off until the tutor is done, and the session state reads Thinking. The caption line shows the question in muted ink, so the learner sees it was heard. The board stays empty.

4. Speaking

Each sentence the tutor says replaces the caption, in ink, and stays for its length at 15 characters per second at 1.5× (this one, 58 characters, for about 3.9 s). Nothing is spoken aloud yet and the board stays empty: drawing arrives in Rabbithole-brd.2, the voice in Rabbithole-brd.7. The learner can type meanwhile; sending to cut in comes in Rabbithole-brd.10.

6. Back in chat: the board turn is an ordinary reply

The question and the tutor's sentences are saved as a learner message and a reply, and the chat view draws them as it draws any turn. Here the learner switched the board off during the fourth sentence. The tutor stopped at once, and the reply keeps every sentence that had started showing.

7. Notice: the tutor stopped

The tutor's process ended on its own during the third sentence. A notice sits at the top centre of the board, over it, so nothing below moves. The reply is saved up to the caption shown, which stays in muted ink, and the session goes idle. Asking again starts a new process. The notice stays until the next question.
