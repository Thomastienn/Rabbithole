What does the dashed arrow on slide 14 mean, and does my worksheet match it?

The dashed arrow is phosphorus moving back to the root. It is dashed because the slide means it is conditional: the fungus sends it only while sugar keeps arriving. Your worksheet’s third trial fits this. Sugar uptake drops after day 9, and phosphorus falls with it.
