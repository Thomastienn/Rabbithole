Why trade at all?

Tutor · Claude · Opus 4

Neither partner can make what the other offers. A plant fixes carbon from air and light, but its roots touch only a thin sleeve of soil. A fungus cannot photosynthesise, yet its threads reach phosphorus the plant never could. Could either manage without the other1.1.1? Rarely, and poorly.

What each side pays

  • The plant pays in sugar, up to a fifth of what it makes.
  • The fungus pays in phosphorus and water, and both sides punish partners that underpay1.1.2.
const trade = { give: "sugar", get: "phosphorus" };

The price follows supply: when soil phosphorus is scarce, the plant pays more sugar per unit, roughly p = k / [P].

Show me how much further hyphae reach than roots.

Tutor · Claude · Opus 4

A root hair reaches about two millimetres into the soil. Hyphae are a tenth as wide and run for centimetres, so the same sugar buys far more surface.
